How to read a creature
  • Size: bigger body. Stores more energy, costs more to keep alive.
  • Speed: longer tail. Twice as fast burns eight times the energy.
  • Sight: bigger eyes. Sees food, mates and danger from farther away.
  • Diet: the head turns red as it eats more meat; past 0.5 it grows orange jaws and can hunt.
  • Crest: a golden fan, males only. Costs energy, slows him down, and hunters notice it. Females choose by it.
  • Taste: invisible, females only. How much a crest matters to her.
  • Colour: the mother's family. It never mutates.
  • Tap or click a creature to read its six numbers. Zoom with the + and − buttons (or pinch / Ctrl + scroll), then drag to move around.
  • A body outlined in red is dead. A teal ring is a birth, an orange ring is a kill.
